Admissions

Begin your child's journey at Solution Souls Schools. Our admission process is simple and straightforward.

How It Works

Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to join the Solution Souls Schools family.

1

Make an Enquiry

Fill out the admission enquiry form below or contact us directly. We'll provide you with all the information you need about our programmes, fees, and requirements.

2

Submit Application

Complete the formal application form and submit the required documents. Our admissions team will guide you through the process.

3

Assessment / Interview

Students will participate in an age-appropriate assessment or interview. This helps us understand your child's needs and ensure we can provide the best learning environment.

4

Admission Decision

After reviewing the assessment results, we will communicate the admission decision. Families will be notified of the outcome within a reasonable timeframe.

5

Enrollment

Complete the enrollment process by submitting final documentation and confirming acceptance. Welcome to Solution Souls Schools!

What You Need

Admission Requirements

Here's what you'll need for the application process. All information is subject to update.

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Student's birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school report card (if applicable)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Parent/Guardian identification document
  • Medical/immunization records

Age / Class Requirements

  • Early Years: Ages 2–5 years
  • Primary School: Ages 6–11 years
  • Secondary School: Ages 12–17 years
  • Age placement is determined by date of birth
  • Transfer students may be placed based on assessment
  • [Age/class requirements subject to confirmation]

Assessment Information

  • Early Years: Informal play-based assessment
  • Primary: Written assessment in English & Maths
  • Secondary: Written assessment in core subjects
  • Interview with parents/guardians
  • Previous school records review
  • [Assessment details subject to confirmation]

Enrollment Information

  • Acceptance letter from the school
  • Payment of admission fees (if applicable)
  • Submission of all required documents
  • School uniform and supply requirements
  • Orientation and settling-in programme
  • [Enrollment details subject to confirmation]
